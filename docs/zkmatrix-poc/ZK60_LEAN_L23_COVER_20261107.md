## 1. In plain words, for Victor

**What this lane is.** Lane L21 proved one block's knowledge statement for ANY block shape: the chance that a block
verifier accepts while the data behind the commitments breaks the block's constraint system is at most your target
`q·2^-100 + 2^-100`. It proved it up to "the instance's algebra": for the profile at hand one must show that every
accepted outcome with a broken slot relation is a win against the slots' random challenges, that a failing lookup is a
looked-up value outside the table, and give the slot polynomials. This lane does that instance for W16R2 (the rung,
8B T128 and Shieldstral T128 shapes) and for R13 (the media app's classifier blocks).

**What is now proved.**
1. **The W16R2 and R13 block statements, assembled** (`block_knowledge_target_w16r2`, `_w16r2_shape`, `_r13`). For the
   W16R2 rung, for any W16R2 shape whose draws and schedule are the code's formulas (with the 8B and Shieldstral facts
   checked), and for the R13 rung: the probability is at most your target for every number of hash queries, GIVEN the
   premises listed in §5 (these statements are not yet unconditional). Inside them the schedules of challenges, their
   degrees, their distinctness, the counts and the whole budget are discharged
   (`30,263,665` of the `2^26 = 67,108,864` units at the W16R2 rung, about `2.2×` margin; at most `31.8` million at
   any W16R2 shape, the 8B and Shieldstral T128 shapes included; at most `38.6` million at the R13 rung for any R13
   opening geometry within `2^-105` per round).
2. **The slots' identities as polynomials of their challenges, for every W16R2 slot kind** (`SlotPoly`): each host,
   standalone zero-check, private slot, `SC_RS`, `SC_ZS`, with its degree and the fact that it vanishes identically only
   when the slot's relation holds. The hosts' challenge vectors are parsed at EVERY W16R2 shape, the private kinds' on
   their draw vectors, and the rung's shape reproduces, by the code's formulas, exactly the challenge counts and
   schedule the code recorded.
3. **The unmasking on acceptance** (`unmask_slots`): from what the verifier accepted and what `M` checks, the masks and
   the prover's scalars drop out: a standalone zero-check's true sum is `0` and a host's is its hosted slots'. This is
   generic in the block shape.
4. **L13's slot builders at every slot**: the number the verifier computes for each slot's polynomial, read in the
   field, is the slot's named form (L13 did this for one slot), and the polynomial the builder makes is canonical.

**Two findings change what the remaining step needs.**
- **L23-1 (Medium, now fixed): the Lean verifier and the code disagreed on one weight vector.** In the host slots'
  mask functional, the code weights each hosted slot's mask with that slot's own sum weights; L9's Lean verifier
  (`ExecBlockVerify`, one line) used the host's. They differ at every W16R2 host, so the Lean verifier would have
  rejected honest proofs and the unmasking could not be completed at the hosts. The coordinator authorised the
  one-line fix; with it every mask cancels (`unmask_slots`), and a new agreement check compares the functionals'
  weights with the code's on a real receipt (`CoverW16MCheck.lean`), so this kind of slip is now caught by a test.
- **L23-2 (Low): two challenge schedules are not enough at W16R2.** Three private slots (`SC_KMM`, `SC_VMM`, `SC_UMM`)
  reuse challenge coordinates another slot drew. Four schedules cover everything; proved, and still within budget.

**What is not done** (§5): the W16R2 slot definitions as a concrete Lean `define` (with the private tables' layout),
from which the slot polynomials over the extracted data and their vanishing on acceptance follow; the reindexing of the
slots' cube relations to the layout theorem's named relations; the lookup list's bridge; and L22's openings link.

## 4. Findings

**L23-1 (Medium, FIXED in this lane's second round): the host `Γ` functional's hosted weights.**
- CODE (argument.rs:1497-1502): a host's `M` functional is `(s, sum_weights(sh, s)·c) :: [(hs, sum_weights(sh, hs)·
  (−κ·c_hs))]`: each hosted slot's mask is weighted with THAT slot's sum weights (its own `(n + e, d)`).
- Lean, before the fix (`ExecBlockVerify.slotAcc`, line 316): the hosted terms used `Cfs.sumWeights (n + e) d` of
  the HOST slot `s`.
- They differ at every W16R2 host (`SC_DZC` `(14, 1, 5)` hosts `SC_WO`, `SC_DMM` `(10, 1, 2)` at the rung; the weight
  lists have different lengths and values). So the Lean block verifier is not the code's on `M`'s input: it would refuse
  an honest receipt (the hosted masks are random), and on its acceptance the unmasking leaves the extra terms
  `c_h·(mdot_h(sw_h) − mdot_h(sw_host))`, which `hRel`'s define schedules cannot see (`c_h` is not in `define`'s
  history): `hvR`'s layer 0 was not dischargeable at the hosts on that model.
- Not caught before: L9's and L17's agreement checks run `M` through a transcript stand-in that ignores the
  functionals, and check only their count (`ExecBlockCheck.lean:318`, `CheckR13Block.lean:317`).
- Fix (applied, authorised by the coordinator; the diff is `results/zk60_lean_l23_20261107/l23_1_proposed_fix.diff`):
  the hosted terms use `slotShape P hk.1`. `SlotFns`'s hosted weight is now `swk P hk.1`, and `unmask_slots`' host
  conclusion is the clean `Sp_host = Σ_h κ_h·Sp_h` (the extra terms cancel: `mdot_h(sw_h) − mdot_h(sw_h) = 0`). On the
  merged tree the fix broke only `slotsLoop_fns` (its host functional no longer matched the stated one, a `whnf`
  timeout); it is repaired by stating the hosted weights as the slot's own and unfolding `swk` in the `simp only` step.
- The test (`CoverW16MCheck.lean`, `mcheck.sh`): L9's executable block verifier, on the real rung core of L9's corpus,
  computes `acc.fs ++ acc.hostFs`; L10's corpus (the SAME receipt: the same `M` root) records the functionals the
  code's verifier handed to `M`. The check compares the count, every term's slot and every weight word, and every `Γ`
  functional's value; its negative control re-weights each host functional's hosted terms with the host's own sum
  weights (the model before the fix) and requires each to differ from the code's (§6).

**L23-2 (Low, fixed here): two define schedules do not cover W16R2's `hRel` event.**
- CODE (L11's `claimPoint`): `SC_KMM` and `SC_VMM` publish `(r_q of SC_QMM, r_j)`, `SC_UMM` `(r_tf of SC_GMM, r_j)`.
  The Schwartz–Zippel escape over `r_q` / `r_tf` for those slots' relations happens at `SC_QMM`'s / `SC_GMM`'s define
  step, against a LATER slot's relation. A define schedule carries one polynomial per slot, so L21's two schedules
  (hosts and standalone zero-checks; private slots) cannot express it: a private slot's start claim at its own step can
  only be the partial claim at the earlier `r` (`privPartGd`), which does not give its pointwise relation.
- Fix: two more layers at the drawing slots (`crossPhi`, §3) and `block_knowledge_target_mtN` (any number of
  schedules). Cost `+121,856` of the `2^26` units; the target still holds with about `2.2×` margin.

**L23-3 (Info): L11's `sh8B` / `shShield` leave the helper counts at `0`.** They enter no numerator (L11), but they set
the draw counts: by L11's formulas `sh8B`'s draws are not the code's. `sh8BW` / `shShieldW` use the layout export's
counts (`layout.txt`: `L` 1, `D` 10, `Q` 3, `Kv` 3, `S` 4, `F` 4, 12 range sub-planes). The rung's (`shRungW16`) reproduce
the code's recorded draws and schedule exactly (`rungDraws_eq_w16`, `rungSched_eq_w16`); at 8B and Shieldstral there is

**L23-4 (Info): the hosted `SC_RS` term sums over the column bits.** `SC_SZC`'s `ν_0` term reads `eq(p_ht)` over the row
coordinates only and is summed over the column cube (`rs_host_cubeSum`: a factor `2^b`), matching the code's hosted
coefficient `ν_0·per_row` with `per_row = 2^b` (L11's `hostedCoefs`). An instance's `SC_SZC` polynomial takes `P_0`
with the column coordinates at `1/2` (or scales `h_0`, `M_0` by `2^b`).
