## Acceptance target

**2026-09-19 construction correction:** [D12](ZK60_DECISIONS.md) requires
one block proof under one transcript, with two external endpoints and no
commitment opened twice. The implemented whole-block relation shares its
internal activation directly; it has no separate attention/FFN endpoint seam.
The earlier boundary-equality wording is superseded by that construction.
This remains a tiny engineering milestone; whole-proof privacy and the
complete model-scale goal are not established by it.

The next implementation gate remains **one tiny fixed-calibration P1 transformer
block**, T32/d16/f16, with hidden input, output and intermediate activations,
independently approved public weights, attention and FFN linked in the same relation,
and saved bytes verified in an independent process. This is an engineering
milestone toward the model-scale MVP, not a replacement for the 8B/model-coverage
goal in the execution contract. The complete verifier view
must meet the stated privacy requirements. The public two-block demo and the
experimental private FFN component do not satisfy this gate.

This block milestone precedes a private two-block chain and the complete
generated-response relation. P3 additionally hides weights and authenticates
the approved private model. GPU optimization and target throughput are later
acceptance gates, and are not substitutes for missing relation coverage.
