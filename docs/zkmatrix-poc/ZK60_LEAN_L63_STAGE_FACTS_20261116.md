## 1. In plain words, for Victor

- The two stages around the blocks (IN-C and OUT-C) each draw random challenges inside their slots ("define draws").
  The top theorem assumed, without proof, that a certain polynomial of those draws is zero at the drawn values
  (`hDI_stg`, `hDO_stg`), and it left the polynomial itself free (`ΦI`, `ΦO`).
- This lane fixes the polynomial to the real one: each slot's sum over the hypercube of its table side, read off the
  committed data, minus what the slot hosts. Lean then **proves** it vanishes at the drawn values, on every accepted
  run where no slot started from a false claim and the mask checks are true. Both premises are gone from the new top
  theorem `ssh_productionReadyL_epP_def`.
- The price: one honest gap in the old stage facts (finding L63-1: they forgot the host mask functionals that the code
  does check), now an explicit premise `hHostO`; and four small set-up facts about fixed objects.
- Not done: the stage dooms, the LogUp states, and `hdzero` (deliverables 2 to 4). Section 5 says exactly what they need.
