## Complete-block experiment

`zkvm-block/` compiles with pinned RISC Zero `=3.0.6`, the installed guest
Rust 1.97.0 toolchain, and locked dependencies. It reexecutes both norms,
QKV, RoPE, causal scores/softmax/PV, O projection, both residuals and SiLU FFN.
No supplied intermediate or claimed output substitutes for that computation.

The experiment uses a new SHA-256 endpoint profile. A 144-byte public journal
binds the profile, caller context, approved model/table digest and two salted
endpoint roots. Shape and integer calibration are fixed by the approved
program image. The verifier compares the complete journal to independently
supplied policy bytes and verifies the compiled image ID. Old PCS endpoints
are not compatible. Model-scale, chained-block and generated-token claims
remain false.

The local owner prepares the policy before proving. Its request, salts and
plaintext output are stored in a separate directory with private permissions;
they do not enter the public artifact directory. The prover uses an explicit
local `r0vm` process. Fake receipts are refused and dev mode is forced off.

The first proof pipeline currently uses a succinct STARK receipt as
experimental evidence, **not accepted whole-proof privacy**. Primary vendor
documentation states an unfinished mathematical ZK argument and approximately
96/99-bit conjectured soundness for the segment/recursion layers:
https://dev.risczero.com/api/security-model . A Groth16 wrapper is being
evaluated to give the public artifact an established outer ZK construction,
with its own setup and implementation premises explicitly reviewed.
