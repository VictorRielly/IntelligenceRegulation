# The Automorphism (Trace) Gate: Design Round

Status: design + algebra spike complete; protocol composition is the next
build tranche. Prototype: `ring_matmul gate-spike` (every identity below
is asserted numerically at (k = 8, q8_31), with tampered-C negative
controls). Companions: ROUTE_R_BUILD_REPORT.md (findings F2/F3),
ROUTE_R_DESIGN.md Sections 2/4 (the ct-engine rules this design obeys).

## 1. The problem being solved (finding F2)

The Pi2 gate must tie `ct(P_ij) = C[i][j]` for all pairs, where
`P_ij := sum_u conj(apack_iu) bpack_ju` are the pair products of the
committed rows/columns. Ring-challenge compression forms
`F = sum_ij conj(s_i) v_j P_ij`, but `ct(w P) != w-weighted ct(P)`: the
ring weights mix P's junk (shift-correlation) coefficients into the ct,
and the C-side commitments carry only the true entries — nothing to
match the junk against. The scalar-challenge fallback costs r_g = 64
repetitions (the r = 1 lever lost). This round restores r = 1.

## 2. The construction (spike-validated)

Gal(R_q) = { sigma_t : X -> X^t, t odd mod 2D }, order D = 128,
generated by 5 and conj = sigma_{2D-1}. For a subgroup H of order h, the
partial trace `T(y) := sum_{sigma in H} sigma(y)` kills a monomial X^e
exactly when its H-orbit picks up a sign flip (t e = e + D mod 2D) —
sign-cancellation. Killed set `K(H) := { e != 0 : T(X^e) = 0 }`.

**Twisted executions.** For each sigma in H the prover forms combined
objects with TWISTED CHALLENGES on UNTWISTED packings (sigma(s) is still
quinary — a valid small combination):

    xa^sigma := sum_i sigma(s_i) apack_i,
    xb^sigma := sum_j sigma(v_j) bpack_j,
    xc^sigma := sum_j sigma(v_j) cpack_j.

**The gate identities** (all verified in the spike, including at the
dense full-group point):

    G_A := sum_{sigma in H} sigma^{-1}( conj(xa^sigma) xb^sigma )
         = sum_ij conj(s_i) v_j T(P_ij),
    G_C := sum_{sigma in H} sigma^{-1}( conj(shat^sigma) xc^sigma ),
    shat^sigma := sum_i sigma(s_i) X^{pos_i}  (public).

When every junk exponent of the pair products lies in K(H) — either by
support-constrained packing, or FOR FREE at the full group where
K = everything — T(P_ij) collapses to the constant h * C[i][j], and:

    G_A = G_C = h * sum_ij conj(s_i) v_j C[i][j],

with the challenge weights OUTSIDE the trace, junk never reaching the
comparison. G_A = G_C as a RING equation iff the weighted defect matrix
vanishes; per-slot Schwartz-Zippel over (s, v) gives 2 * 2^-296-class
knowledge error at r = 1. This is the identity finding F2 said the naive
gate lacked; the trace supplies it.

## 3. The frontier law (measured)

The spike tabulates, for the subgroup tower <5^(64/h)> (+- conj), the
killed sets and the maximum packing density with all pairwise position
differences killed. The result is a clean invariant:

    (twisted executions h) x (1 / packing density) = D = 128, everywhere.

| H | h | density | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| <5^16, conj> | 8 | 1/16 | verified end-to-end |
| <5^8, conj> | 16 | 1/8 | verified end-to-end |
| <5^2, conj> | 64 | 1/2 | half-density, the F2-analysis point |
| **full group** | **128** | **1.0** | **verified end-to-end; RECOMMENDED** |

The product is the information-theoretic price of extracting one clean
coefficient from the packed bilinear structure; the design freedom is
only how to split it between claim-layer executions and commit-density.

**Recommended point: the full group (h = 128, density 1.0).** Dense
packing means ZERO commit-bill penalty and NO support claims — the
commit side (the dominant global cost, A1 kernels) is completely
untouched. The entire price sits in the claim layer as 128 twisted
executions, which is where amortization machinery exists.

## 4. Cost model and the amortization lever

Per matmul claim at the full group, the naive claim-layer cost is 3h =
384 combined openings (each a digit bundle) — that would be ~11 MB-class,
no better than the scalar gate. The lever is ALS20-style AMORTIZED
openings: the 384 combos are linear images of one witness family, so one
masked response can open a batch of N with norm growth ~sqrt(N), i.e.
+0.5 log2(N) bits on beta. Against the frozen C2 headroom (q31, e = 2:
2.9 bits):

| batching | extra bits | q31 e=2 | bundles/claim | est. size/claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 0 | fits | 384 | ~11 MB (no win) |
| batches of 8 | +1.5 | fits | 48 | ~1.4 MB (10x over target) |
| batches of 16 | +2.0 | fits (0.9 spare) | 24 | ~0.7 MB |
| single bundle | +4.3 | does NOT fit | 1-2 | ~100 KB-class (target) |

Three candidate resolutions for the single-bundle point, ranked:

1. **Digit-decompose the amortized response itself** (Q2 applied
   recursively): the amortized z has larger coefficients; decomposing z
   into base-2^e digits keeps beta at digit scale regardless of N — the
   existing digit machinery, one more level. Leading candidate; the
   recombination-check composition (per-digit equations against the
   amortized RHS `w + sum c_k T_k`) needs one page of derivation in the
   build tranche. If it composes, the gate lands at ~100-200 KB/claim
   and r = 1 — the design-target class.
2. **Gate layer at q46** with dual-modulus links for the combos: the
   +4.3 bits are trivial at q46, but the combos combine q31 commitments,
   so a modulus-link argument is needed — machinery SHARED with the F3
   resolution (the binary-gadget side condition), one design item
   serving both.
3. **Batches of 16 at q31** (works TODAY, no new machinery): ~0.7 MB and
   ~24 bundles per claim — an ~18x improvement over the scalar gate,
   ~6x over target. Acceptable interim.

## 5. Protocol composition sketch (the build tranche's checklist)

- The h twisted quadratic products ride the EXISTING quadratic-claim
  layer (U6): each `conj(xa^sigma) xb^sigma` is a sigma-twisted quadratic
  monomial over combined commitments; the verifier applies sigma^{-1} to
  each claim's ring-valued verification identity (automorphisms are free
  on public equations) and checks the SUMMED identity G_A - G_C = 0 —
  one new engine feature: "traced claim sums" (sum of sigma^{-1}-twisted
  claim equations checked against a single prover-supplied garbage
  aggregate, not h separate ones).
- Challenge schedule: one (s, v) pair per claim; the h twists are
  DETERMINISTIC functions of it (no fresh challenges) — soundness comes
  from the one SZ event, matching Section 2's accounting.
- The 3h combos: one amortized opening per side per batch (Section 4);
  digit statements published per batch, recombination against
  verifier-computed combined T1s (the U4/U5 pattern).
- ZK note (Q-A2 below): the twists reuse (s, v), so the h executions
  reveal h correlated combinations of the same witnesses; the mask
  accounting must cover the aggregate (expected: the standard
  simulation, since all reveals are challenge-images of masked objects,
  but it must be WRITTEN OUT — same status as the Q4 garbage-term
  lemma).
