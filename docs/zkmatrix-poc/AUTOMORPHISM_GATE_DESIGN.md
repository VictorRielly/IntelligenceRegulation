# The Automorphism (Trace) Gate: Design Round

Status: design + algebra spike complete; protocol composition is the next
build tranche. Prototype: `ring_matmul gate-spike` (every identity below
is asserted numerically at (k = 8, q8_31), with tampered-C negative
controls). Companions: ROUTE_R_BUILD_REPORT.md (findings F2/F3),
ROUTE_R_DESIGN.md Sections 2/4 (the ct-engine rules this design obeys).

## 1. The problem being solved (finding F2)

The Pi2 gate must tie `ct(P_ij) = C[i][j]` for all pairs, where
`P_ij := sum_u conj(apack_iu) bpack_ju` are the pair products of the
committed rows/columns. Ring-challenge compression forms
`F = sum_ij conj(s_i) v_j P_ij`, but `ct(w P) != w-weighted ct(P)`: the
ring weights mix P's junk (shift-correlation) coefficients into the ct,
and the C-side commitments carry only the true entries — nothing to
match the junk against. The scalar-challenge fallback costs r_g = 64
repetitions (the r = 1 lever lost). This round restores r = 1.

## 2. The construction (spike-validated)

Gal(R_q) = { sigma_t : X -> X^t, t odd mod 2D }, order D = 128,
generated by 5 and conj = sigma_{2D-1}. For a subgroup H of order h, the
partial trace `T(y) := sum_{sigma in H} sigma(y)` kills a monomial X^e
exactly when its H-orbit picks up a sign flip (t e = e + D mod 2D) —
sign-cancellation. Killed set `K(H) := { e != 0 : T(X^e) = 0 }`.

**Twisted executions.** For each sigma in H the prover forms combined
objects with TWISTED CHALLENGES on UNTWISTED packings (sigma(s) is still
quinary — a valid small combination):

    xa^sigma := sum_i sigma(s_i) apack_i,
    xb^sigma := sum_j sigma(v_j) bpack_j,
    xc^sigma := sum_j sigma(v_j) cpack_j.

**The gate identities** (all verified in the spike, including at the
dense full-group point):

    G_A := sum_{sigma in H} sigma^{-1}( conj(xa^sigma) xb^sigma )
         = sum_ij conj(s_i) v_j T(P_ij),
    G_C := sum_{sigma in H} sigma^{-1}( conj(shat^sigma) xc^sigma ),
    shat^sigma := sum_i sigma(s_i) X^{pos_i}  (public).

When every junk exponent of the pair products lies in K(H) — either by
support-constrained packing, or FOR FREE at the full group where
K = everything — T(P_ij) collapses to the constant h * C[i][j], and:

    G_A = G_C = h * sum_ij conj(s_i) v_j C[i][j],

with the challenge weights OUTSIDE the trace, junk never reaching the
comparison. G_A = G_C as a RING equation iff the weighted defect matrix
vanishes; per-slot Schwartz-Zippel over (s, v) gives 2 * 2^-296-class
knowledge error at r = 1. This is the identity finding F2 said the naive
gate lacked; the trace supplies it.

## 3. The frontier law (measured)

The spike tabulates, for the subgroup tower <5^(64/h)> (+- conj), the
killed sets and the maximum packing density with all pairwise position
differences killed. The result is a clean invariant:

    (twisted executions h) x (1 / packing density) = D = 128, everywhere.

| H | h | density | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| <5^16, conj> | 8 | 1/16 | verified end-to-end |
| <5^8, conj> | 16 | 1/8 | verified end-to-end |
| <5^2, conj> | 64 | 1/2 | half-density, the F2-analysis point |
| **full group** | **128** | **1.0** | **verified end-to-end; RECOMMENDED** |

The product is the information-theoretic price of extracting one clean
coefficient from the packed bilinear structure; the design freedom is
only how to split it between claim-layer executions and commit-density.

**Recommended point: the full group (h = 128, density 1.0).** Dense
packing means ZERO commit-bill penalty and NO support claims — the
commit side (the dominant global cost, A1 kernels) is completely
untouched. The entire price sits in the claim layer as 128 twisted
executions, which is where amortization machinery exists.

## 4. Cost model and the amortization lever

Per matmul claim at the full group, the naive claim-layer cost is 3h =
384 combined openings (each a digit bundle) — that would be ~11 MB-class,
no better than the scalar gate. The lever is ALS20-style AMORTIZED
openings: the 384 combos are linear images of one witness family, so one
masked response can open a batch of N with norm growth ~sqrt(N), i.e.
+0.5 log2(N) bits on beta. Against the frozen C2 headroom (q31, e = 2:
2.9 bits):

| batching | extra bits | q31 e=2 | bundles/claim | est. size/claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 0 | fits | 384 | ~11 MB (no win) |
| batches of 8 | +1.5 | fits | 48 | ~1.4 MB (10x over target) |
| batches of 16 | +2.0 | fits (0.9 spare) | 24 | ~0.7 MB |
| single bundle | +4.3 | does NOT fit | 1-2 | ~100 KB-class (target) |

Three candidate resolutions for the single-bundle point, ranked:

1. **Digit-decompose the amortized response itself** (Q2 applied
   recursively): the amortized z has larger coefficients; decomposing z
   into base-2^e digits keeps beta at digit scale regardless of N — the
   existing digit machinery, one more level. Leading candidate; the
   recombination-check composition (per-digit equations against the
   amortized RHS `w + sum c_k T_k`) needs one page of derivation in the
   build tranche. If it composes, the gate lands at ~100-200 KB/claim
   and r = 1 — the design-target class.
2. **Gate layer at q46** with dual-modulus links for the combos: the
   +4.3 bits are trivial at q46, but the combos combine q31 commitments,
   so a modulus-link argument is needed — machinery SHARED with the F3
   resolution (the binary-gadget side condition), one design item
   serving both.
3. **Batches of 16 at q31** (works TODAY, no new machinery): ~0.7 MB and
   ~24 bundles per claim — an ~18x improvement over the scalar gate,
   ~6x over target. Acceptable interim.

## 5. Protocol composition sketch (the build tranche's checklist)

- The h twisted quadratic products ride the EXISTING quadratic-claim
  layer (U6): each `conj(xa^sigma) xb^sigma` is a sigma-twisted quadratic
  monomial over combined commitments; the verifier applies sigma^{-1} to
  each claim's ring-valued verification identity (automorphisms are free
  on public equations) and checks the SUMMED identity G_A - G_C = 0 —
  one new engine feature: "traced claim sums" (sum of sigma^{-1}-twisted
  claim equations checked against a single prover-supplied garbage
  aggregate, not h separate ones).
- Challenge schedule: one (s, v) pair per claim; the h twists are
  DETERMINISTIC functions of it (no fresh challenges) — soundness comes
  from the one SZ event, matching Section 2's accounting.
- The 3h combos: one amortized opening per side per batch (Section 4);
  digit statements published per batch, recombination against
  verifier-computed combined T1s (the U4/U5 pattern).
- ZK note (Q-A2 below): the twists reuse (s, v), so the h executions
  reveal h correlated combinations of the same witnesses; the mask
  accounting must cover the aggregate (expected: the standard
  simulation, since all reveals are challenge-images of masked objects,
  but it must be WRITTEN OUT — same status as the Q4 garbage-term
  lemma).

## Appendix A — the U-A0 derivation (Q-A1 resolved; the tranche's outcome)

### A.1 The ya-reformulation: the claim layer collapses to O(1)

Since sigma^{-1} is a ring automorphism and commutes with conj:

    G_A = sum_sigma conj(ya^sigma) * yb^sigma,   ya^sigma := sigma^{-1}(xa^sigma)
        = sum_i s_i * sigma^{-1}(apack_i),

i.e. UNtwisted challenge weights over sigma-twisted commitment images.
sigma^{-1}(com(x)) is a valid commitment to sigma^{-1}(x) under the
twisted CRS sigma^{-1}(A1, A2) with randomness sigma^{-1}(rho) (still
ternary; automorphisms are coefficient permutations up to sign, so all
norms and challenge bounds are preserved). The verifier computes the
twisted commitments and CRSs for free. G_A is then ONE quadratic claim
with h monomials — the existing engine's multi-monomial expansion
already yields a SINGLE garbage triple (Ga, Gb, G0) and ONE W element
for the whole aggregate, and the C side is LINEAR (public coefficients
conj(sigma^{-1}(shat^sigma))). Claim-layer data: O(1) ring elements
per matmul claim. This part is a clean win.

### A.2 The response-volume floor: amortization cannot cross the P-table

The quadratic expansion consumes per-object verifier-computable parts
P^sigma = c * T2^sigma - A2^sigma z^sigma. An ALS20-amortized opening
(one masked response for a batch) makes the individual A2^sigma z^sigma
unavailable, and every attempted repair fails structurally:

- summing per-sigma verification identities twisted by sigma^{-1}
  leaves per-sigma A2^sigma z^sigma terms that do not aggregate into
  one matvec (A2 sits inside sigma^{-1} per term);
- sharing one Gaussian mask across the h responses breaks the
  rejection-sampling ZK argument (mask reuse leaks challenge-weighted
  witness differences);
- revealing the combined messages or the pair products leaks the
  witness or reintroduces per-pair objects (the F2 lesson).

So every object referenced by a quadratic monomial must carry its own
response vector. At h = 128: 3h = 384 response bundles per claim —
about 2x the scalar gate's 3 r_g = 192 at r_g = 64, for a comparable
soundness grade (2^-296 per-slot vs 2^-256). The subgroup frontier
cannot rescue this: scanning further subgroups (e.g. <3>, which kills
125 of 127 exponents at order 64) reproduces h x (1/density) = d
exactly — the law appears structural (a character-sum identity), so
trading executions against density never changes the product.

**Verdict: the h = 128 trace gate is NOT the right default today.** It
is retained as the substrate for A.3 and for its soundness margin, but
the scalar gate remains the shipping gate this tranche.

### A.3 Q-A5 — the recursive gate: the real rung-1 path

The diagonal-extraction obstacle is itself a bilinear gate: with
FS-drawn aggregation weights omega, the cross-term matrix
<ya^sigma, yb^sigma'> is an h x h analogue of the original rows x cols
problem — and the SAME trace construction applies to it. Recursing
rows x cols -> h x h -> ... with geometric shrinkage gives a
polylog-depth, LaBRADOR-shaped argument whose terminal level opens O(1)
objects. Norm growth per level, ZK per level, and the FS schedule are
open; this is a full design round (entry criterion: equations first,
against the LaBRADOR/ALS20 amortization literature). Target: the
~100-200 KB rung the design Section 4 hoped for.

### A.4 What this tranche ships instead (proven wins)

1. **Joint-scaling rejection** (Q4_LEMMAS.md Q4.2b, PROVEN): one
   acceptance over the jointly-scaled bundles cuts expected round
   attempts 21.1 -> 5.7 at identical sigmas — a ~3.7x prover-time win
   on every multi-bundle round in the protocol.
2. **The derived gate-scalar width** (`derive_gate_b`): the largest b
   whose combination digit chain fits the modulus with C2 headroom AND
   C3 binding rows at the shape's kappa, scanning digit widths
   e in {4, 3, 2} per b and minimizing the HONEST cost metric
   r_g x L (response bundles per gate), not max b. The chain (not the
   hand estimate) decides: demo shape **b = 8, e = 4 -> r_g = 43,
   cost 172 vs 256 at b = 6 (a 33% cut)**; FFN shape **b = 6, e = 3 ->
   r_g = 64** — the b-lever is a demo-scale win only, which is honest

3. **Q4.3c fix R1** (garbage-term masking): additive one-time mask

## Appendix B — Q-A5 resolved: the committed-executions gate (depth-1)

The recursion terminates after ONE level. The insight: the reason the
trace gate cost 3h openings was that the twisted execution objects were
DERIVED (challenge combinations), and every object referenced by a
quadratic claim must carry its own opening. But the execution vector has
only h = 128 entries — small enough to RE-COMMIT wholesale:

1. After (s, v) are drawn, the prover commits the execution vectors
       YA := (sigma^{-1}(xa^sigma))_{sigma in Gal},  YB, YC
   as fresh commitments with h = 128 message slots each (plain ternary
   randomness — ordinary openings, no digits).
2. ONE tau-batched tie claim per vector binds it to the originals: with
   fresh small ring challenges tau (drawn after the YA commitment),
       sum_sigma tau_sigma YA_sigma  =  W_A
       := sum_i s_i [ sum_sigma tau_sigma sigma^{-1}(apack_i) ],
   where W_A is a verifier-computable combination of TWISTED ORIGINAL
   commitments with small product coefficients (s_i tau_sigma) — one
   digit-openable object. Any wrong YA entry is caught per-slot
   (2^-296). Spike-verified ("tau-tie" assertion).
3. The gate is then ONE sigma-twisted ring-valued quadratic claim over
   the fresh commitments:
       sum_sigma conj(YA_sigma) YB_sigma
         - sum_sigma conj(sigma^{-1}(shat^sigma)) YC_sigma = 0,
   full ring, no reveal, r = 1. Spike-verified dense at h = 128 with the
   tampered-C negative.

No twisted-CRS kernel machinery is needed anywhere: the Galois twists
act only on public data (the W combinations and the shat coefficients).

Cost, CORRECTED at build time (the spike's ~300 KB line was a
msg_r = 1 artifact — with multi-slot rows the execution vectors carry
h * (inner/D) message slots, and BDLOP hiding forces message-sized
randomness, so the opening bundles scale with the INNER dimension):
3 plain bundles at the (h * inner/D)-slot shape + 3 digit-opened tie
combinations + 1 quadratic claim. Estimates: ~350 KB at the demo shape,
~1.7 MB at n = 2048, ~3.3 MB at FFN shapes — **~5-8x below the scalar
gate at full r = 1 per-slot soundness**. MEASURED (U-B2/U-B3,
`self-test` pi2-ce section, demo shape inner = 128): gate MARGINAL cost
345 KB (3 Y commitments 198 KB + 3 Y opening bundles 145 KB + claim
images 2 KB) — the ~350 KB estimate confirmed; whole demo round
(including the pre-existing A/B/C commitments and openings) 688 KB;
prove ~260 ms (A1 kernels) / verify ~75 ms at r = 1, vs
~8.7-12.9 MB for the scalar gate at the same shape. The consequence
split: prove TIME returns to the r = 1 class (bundles are fast to
produce — the time goal is served); proof SIZE at MB-class per claim at
scale means the small-proof deployment target eventually needs the
aggregation/succinctness follow-up (GATE_ALTERNATIVES.md options 2/3).
The W-combination randomness magnitude measures ~2^18.1
(sqrt-extrapolated to n = 2048), i.e. digit chains at L ~ 5 —
comfortably inside q31 (lemma-grade bound is a build-tranche item; the
empirical envelope is now a freeze-check row).

Build tranche (U-B1..U-B4): (B1) engine: public-constant terms in

### Appendix B.1 — the tie integration: an OPEN design item

What exists: the tie is enforced in the demo at witness level (the
verifier-side identity `sum_s tau_s YA_s = sum_i s_i sum_t tau_t
sigma_t^{-1}(apack_i)`, checked on openings, tau drawn AFTER the Y
commitments). What is missing: the tie as a first-class claim inside
the masked ct-round, referencing only commitments. An earlier draft of
this appendix asserted this was "designed" with the verifier doing "h
twisted A2 matvecs, proof unchanged" — that was an overclaim, retracted
here; the derivation below records what is actually known.

The obstruction (found in review, 2026-07-09). The round's virtual
opening P_i = c x_i - Y_i carries the round challenge c. Any tie
formulation puts a Galois twist on at least one COMMITTED object
(YA-side or original-side — the twist is conserved across the
equation), and the twisted virtual opening sigma_t(P) = sigma_t(c)
sigma_t(x) - sigma_t(Y) carries the twisted challenge monomial
sigma_t(c). A claim mixing twist classes therefore mixes challenge
monomials {c, sigma_t(c)}, and a single check equation with
pre-challenge prover images pins witness combinations PER MONOMIAL —
but the tie is one equation across monomials, so it cannot be checked
as a plain multi-twist linear claim. Two specific dead ends, recorded:
(a) per-twist reveal pivots u_t = sigma_t(YA_t) + m_t with the mask
committed on one side need the mask consistency m_t = sigma_t(mu_t),
which is itself a mixed-twist claim — circular; (b) unmasked reveals
of sigma_t(YA_t) reopen the executions (the ZK regression this design
exists to avoid).

The candidate route (to be derived on paper first, U-A0 discipline):
masked per-twist reveals E_t = U_t + m_t with ONE untwisted mask
commitment M = (m_t), where U_t := tau_t sum_i s_i sigma_t^{-1}(x_a,i).
Substituting the m_t virtual openings into the check turns the tie into
a claim with challenge monomials sigma_t^{-1}(c) * c — the same
monomial family the twisted QUADRATIC layer (B1) already handles with
pre-challenge garbage images. Expected cost: ~h ring elements of
reveals + one h-slot mask commitment per tie (~64-70 KB per side at the
demo shape — the proof does NOT stay unchanged), verifier does h cheap
entrywise twists of already-computed matvec results. Soundness and ZK
of this composition are exactly what the paper derivation must
establish before any engine code (the U-A0 lesson: derive first, build
second).
