L350: ## Measured serving and memory profiles
L351: 
L352: The serving experiments send complete encoded proofs over private TCP to a separate generator/verifier frontend. L354:   * Two connections to the same 192-core server: 15-second screens met one second at every rate from 3,400 to 5,000 requests/s. At 5,000/s the 48-core frontend reached its own limit, so 5,000/s is a lower bound for the server.
L355:   * 192 cores, 1,000 requests/s: p99 33.7–33.9 ms in three 60-second runs, against 433.8–475.1 ms for v1.1.0 on the same day.
L356:   * 48 and 96 cores: in screens with one 15-second run per rate, the highest offered rate with every timed request accepted within one second was 2,000 and 3,000 requests/s; these were not confirmed by longer runs.
