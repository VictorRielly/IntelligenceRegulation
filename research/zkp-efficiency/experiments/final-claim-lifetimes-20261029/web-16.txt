# 25. Sumcheck is intentionally dense

`lattice-sumcheck` is an excellent correctness oracle, but it clones and folds dense tables.

Its cost profile is not what a production Jolt prover needs.

Important simplifications include materializing every factor over the full hypercube, re-folding full vectors each round, generic interpolation over `0..degree` with nested loops, no zero-copy layer views, no rayon parallelism, no GPU backend, no structured polynomial evaluation, no streaming sumcheck, and no shared batch-sumcheck engine.

This is one of the most important performance layers to optimize after protocol correctness is frozen.

Keep the current implementation permanently as the reference backend.
