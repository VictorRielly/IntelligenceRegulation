L171: # StateSync-GKR v1.2.1
L172: StateSync-GKR v1.2.1 attaches an external zkVM proof of the v1.2 program identity. The library crates, the proof format and the program identity are unchanged from v1.2.0. `statesync-gkr-v1.2-proof.cbor` is a secure succinct RISC Zero proof bound to image ID `1a8b55f4e1a5f703386dcfc07e5e8b2e42b5adade6be3d0057f2d565f75eb131`; the zkVerify RISC0 verifier on the Volta test network accepted it and aggregated it into an authenticated receipt.
L173: The proof manifest, the public inputs and the run record describe the proof, its public statement and the receipt. The v1.1 proof remains a record of the v1.1 program and is not attached to this release; its manifest and public inputs are kept for comparison.
L174: The documentation reports the v1.2.0 measurements against v1.1.0 measured on the same days and instance types: on one 192-core VM, a p99 latency of 34 ms at 1,000 requests/s against 440 ms, and 3,300 requests/s with every request accepted within one second against 1,150/s.
