## Ranked hypotheses and ceilings

1. **Refined using new 8B clocks: authenticated public setup/resident weights.**
   Initial load/digest is ~80 s, and the one-block cache reloads weights in
   proving and verification. Test safe immutable public preprocessing and
   bounded residency before assuming these costs amortize. Eliminate repeated
   parsing/reencoding/digest/IO; add a trusted content identity boundary,
   mutation invalidation and significant retained memory. Every per-proof mask,
   commitment and checked relation must remain fresh. At fixed cold CPU time,
   deleting the first 83.012 s alone caps at 1.275x; it says nothing about a
   resident steady slope. Requires admitted hardware memory and complete repeat
   measurements. Falsified if revalidation/IO or capacity costs erase savings,
   or a changed source can survive a cache hit. Owner heavy experiment only.
2. **Owner D/F zero-check device/driver work.** Earlier 1.1B T128 GPU-assisted
   F consumes 19.484/161.857 s. Its total deletion ceiling is 1.137x; tested
   constant factoring removes only a subset. Charge GPU transfer, scratch,
   folds, extensions and drain. Requires exact field and whole transcript/PCS/
   privacy gates. Reject on any mismatch or no enclosing gain. New 8B F clocks
   are absent; do not transplant the 1.1B fraction or CPU microbenchmark ratio.
3. **Tested, low integration cost: this F arithmetic patch.** Review as a small
   owner candidate; no native end-to-end ceiling within F is known. Even
   deleting ALL new 8B proof-minus-witness/reload work, much more than this
   patch touches, caps cold process ratios at 1.402x CPU / 1.430x GPU-assisted
   with other costs held fixed. It cannot establish >=50 tokens/s. Specific
   falsification: all native gates pass but complete time is unchanged/slower.
4. **New next local question: F grouped equality driver.** Factor its complete
   polynomial into tau/gate-host/up-host brackets, then test the existing
   `GroupedEqRounds` seam against ordinary samples and terminals. Eliminate
   dense equality interpolation/weighting where measured; add group setup,
   brackets and exact degree bookkeeping. No native F subprofile, so speed
   remains unknown. Preserve helper/pad/extension obligations; a changed final
   oracle or extra setup that dominates falsifies it. Do not generalize prior
   grouped-fusion results into success. Borrowed lookup planes stay a demoted
   capacity option; all prior arithmetic/privacy/NTT negatives are preserved.
