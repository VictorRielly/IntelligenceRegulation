No complete private 8B GPU throughput measurement exists yet. The target stays
>=50 proved tokens/s, including witness preparation and serialization;
512-token prefill reference <=10.24 s, five seconds stretch. Longer workloads
need total and warm clocks; prefill does not measure decode.

This cycle tested removing the hiding encoder’s intermediate coefficient
buffer by producing pad/coset terms inside the first NTT load. All 138
synthetic cases, 15 native reference vectors, three larger sparse cases,
chunk/stride checks and 13 negative controls pass. The CPU experiment took
4.06 s and about 20 MiB; it is not a proving benchmark.

The result is mixed: one modeled large row removes 25.15% of logical traffic
in the pad/first-pass interface, but counts 9.55% more memory-sector requests.
Scattered reads can erase the saving. No native speed or complete-readiness
improvement is established, and the candidate is not promoted as a default.
The existing q>n pad-overlap defect remains a preserved negative control.

bytes .91/.82 s separately. This lane rechecked four non-warm log hashes; it
did not rerun the benchmark. Native grouped-fusion work remains with main.
