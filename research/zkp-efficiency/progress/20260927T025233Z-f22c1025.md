# Research progress — cycle 0011, 2026-09-27 UTC

**No complete private 8B GPU throughput result exists.** Victor's target is
>=50 independently verified attention-plus-FFN proved tokens/s on one RTX
5090 or another named high-end GPU, including witness and serialization.
Larger workloads may amortize startup; 512 tokens/10.24 seconds is a reference.
Steady-state and total-workload rates, startup fraction, proof lag, and
long-prefill versus resident-pass versus decode measurements remain unknown.

This cycle tested writing already cached U8 lookup inverses directly into H's
two field planes. An independent Fp2 oracle and the existing materialized
layout matched in **63 synthetic cases**, including **7,932** output words,
19 refusals and eight controls. A plain writer partly mutates the private H
buffer before a late refusal. A guarded writer preserves the old no-partial-H
behavior, with an extra input scan only when the public extension coefficients
match. The one-thread CPU test took **2.097 s**, peak **16,500 KiB** RSS; it
was not a proof benchmark.

At the 125M development shape, source counts suggest removal of **1.555 GiB**
of logical temporary traffic and a **64-MiB** largest modeled helper payload. These
are not DRAM or runtime measurements. Latest owner CPU old-arm measurements
put the entire helper phase at **1.189/1.248 s** of known readiness
**41.462/41.089 s**, so even deleting the whole phase would save only
**2.87–3.04%** there. Actual gain could be zero; this cannot establish the
50-token/s target. Those old-arm diagnostics are T512, **one 125M block, zero
complete-model passes**, shared two-thread CPU: witness **6.74/5.63 s**,
proof **34.697/35.436 s**, serialization **.025/.023 s**, verification
**.88/.87 s** separately. They contribute **zero complete-stack tokens**;
startup fraction and both proved-token rates are unmeasured. No native
receipt, GPU, 8B or larger-profile privacy gate was run by this cycle.

demoted. Keyed GPU commitment correctness passed on RTX 4070 Laptop, without
speed timing. The active chunked GPU PCS already avoids retaining full A/H
