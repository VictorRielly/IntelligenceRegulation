# A24. Which texts fit the installed classifier?

**Short-message studies suggest that most of their texts would fit one chunk per policy. The published forum-comment figures support a much wider range. None of the studies measured its texts with the installed tokenizer.** A whole post can use up to 336 content tokens and 1,000 characters after trimming. Fitting all three policies in one chunk each requires at most 49 content tokens and still produces three proofs. [Installed policy counts](20261126-apps-installed-policy-lengths.md) · [Measured boundary cases](../../../results/zk60_astra_20261026/app_text_capacity_20261126/RESULT.json)

### What the app accepts

MEASURED against the installed W16R2 profile, its retained public pilot statements and the app’s author endpoints. Each native request has 128 positions including the policy template. The sexual-content and violence policies leave 55 positions for content, the hate policy 49. A longer post uses chunks with eight-token overlap. A 50-token test post needed four proofs, a 336-token post needed 22, and 337 tokens exceeded the limit. Two test drafts counted 223 tokens each. The 1,000-character draft passed the preview and the 1,001-character draft failed the submission’s character check. The native export’s `profile.json` (SHA-256 `9464388091d8095f8491f2990fde54e3b1f2b06f2a5c6ad1e72eff09cd4ce20a`) is on the project machine only. The matching app configuration is in the repository. [Native and API checks](../../../results/zk60_astra_20261026/app_text_capacity_20261126/README.md) · [App configuration](../../../apps/proven-media/deploy.json)

### What the published distributions support

DERIVED estimates below use an English approximation of four characters or 0.75 words per token. Under that conversion, the 49-token threshold is 196 characters or 36 whole words. The 55-token threshold is 220 characters or 41 whole words. The 336-token post budget gives 1,344 characters or 252 words, but the separate character check lowers the effective estimate to 1,000 characters or about 187 whole words. A range uses only the published cutoff or quartiles. It describes the **length proxy** for the named sample. The actual Tekken fit rate can fall outside it. [Conversion source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/4936856-what-are-tokens-and-how-to-count-them)

### What would settle the estimate

Two constructed 196-character strings produced **18 and 98 actual content tokens**, requiring three and seven proofs. The real pilot fixture had 62 characters, 13 whitespace-delimited words and 14 tokens. A guest with a nonblank draft can also use a conservative byte rule. The app prepends one ASCII space, then byte-pair encodes without BOS or EOS. Up to 48 UTF-8 content bytes therefore fit the tightest policy, and up to 335 bytes fit the whole post. Many longer texts also fit. [App encoder](../../../apps/proven-media/proven_media/shieldstral_text.py) · [Measured examples](../../../results/zk60_astra_20261026/app_text_capacity_20261126/RESULT.json)

An exact rate needs an authorized representative sample in the intended language and writing form. Run the same trimmed-text check and pinned tokenizer on each item, count the accepted submissions and the one-chunk cases separately, and retain only aggregate counts. I read published papers and aggregate reports for this handoff and downloaded no dataset. These studies give no coverage rate for EU moderation filings or evidence of classifier accuracy.
