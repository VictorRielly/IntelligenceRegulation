# Open-weight screens for machine-written text

A2 asks which detector could produce a verifiable receipt. **GLTR with GPT-2 small is the simplest decoder experiment here.** It needs one 124M-parameter model and a rank count. Binoculars detected more unmodified machine text in the independent comparison, at the cost of two 7B passes and a scoring rule over both vocabulary distributions. None of these detectors runs in the installed prover today. Its trained classifier screens content categories. The two-model methods each fit an 8B **per-pass** ceiling. They require distinct weight imports, linked input commitments and a proved joint reduction. No model here shares the installed classifier’s exact architecture and head. The prover operator sees the text. A prospective private receipt can disclose the threshold result while hiding the text and intermediate scores, subject to the new profile’s correctness and privacy gates. It establishes that the specified screen returned that result. Human or machine authorship remains an inference. RAID evaluated GPTZero, Originality, Winston AI and ZeroGPT as closed services. Their API results supply no weights for a locally replayed proof. ## Times for complete document coverage

MEASURED reference rate on the work Mac is 40.2 padded tokens/s for a warm synthetic W16R2 8B, 32-block T128 stream with fused RS and sparse fold. Its measured statement omits the per-position vocabulary head. ESTIMATE assumes independent 128-position windows, no overlap, one full-coverage pass per model, and equal throughput to that reference for every backbone. It gives no speed credit for smaller models. [Benchmark and switches](../../../results/zk60_composed_8b_20261123/README.md) · [Token-I/O scope](../../../docs/zkmatrix-poc/MVP_STATUS.md#L2495)

| Candidate | Backbone passes | 1,000 input tokens | 10,000 input tokens |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| RoBERTa-large or GLTR | 1 | 25.5 s | 251.5 s |
| Fast-DetectGPT or Binoculars | 2 | 50.9 s | 503.1 s |

DERIVED as passes × 128 × ceil(tokens/128) / 40.2. This prices backbone work only. Special tokens, overlap and carried context can increase work. A 128-token window score with a fixed document aggregation is a new detector whose accuracy must be measured. Proving a default truncated Binoculars verdict would cover at most its first 512 tokens. No full detector proof time, integer accuracy, release-file hash or deployable long-document threshold was verified. The benchmark’s raw GLTR wrapper did not resolve during this review. Its cutoff is sourced to the authors’ paper.

## Deciding test

Freeze the GLTR checkpoint and tokenizer hashes, integer export, rank tie rule, window boundaries and document threshold. Calibrate on a separate human split. On untouched RAID test data compare reference and integer ranks, disagreements at the decision threshold, detection rate and observed human false positives before and after the published text modifications. Time the backbone, full head and rank checks separately. Require complete input coverage and fresh verification to reject a changed token, omitted window or changed threshold. A detector that misses its prespecified operating point cannot support that product claim even if every proof verifies.
