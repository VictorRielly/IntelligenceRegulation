# Which applications can carry today's receipts?

An institution that stores a receipt once and checks it on the same machine has a different size budget from a reader who downloads and verifies every item. I compared the November 24 guest download with the B6a build plan, the B6b study, the trained 8B evaluation and the synthetic image replay. The suggested workflows and tolerances below are product hypotheses.

## Measured baselines and modelled alternatives

- **MEASURED, installed text classifier.** A 14-token public post needed three W16R2 category receipts of 669,561,472 bytes each. The offline verification download was 2,008,826,368 bytes. Its signed manifest alone was 6,264 bytes. On the installed M3 Ultra the three fresh native verifier processes took 42.66, 42.31 and 43.21 seconds of child wall time. DERIVED sum **128.19 seconds**, excluding admission waits, download and separate rehashing. The verifier kit is another 11.95 GB, reusable across posts. Signing and inclusion checks can be done without that kit. [Guest pilot](../../../results/zk60_astra_20261026/app_composed_native_20261122/PILOT.md#a-guest-download-for-whole-post-verification) · [Native kit](../../../results/zk60_astra_20261026/app_composed_native_20261122/PORTABLE_KIT_NATIVE.json)
- **MODEL, B6a build target.** One classifier request is 354.0 MB, or 333.4 MB when the boundary openings also change. DERIVED three requests are **1.062 GB or 1.0002 GB** before packaging. The 8B synthetic stack is 818.7 MB MEASURED and 430.4 or 405.0 MB MODEL, depending on the join. The proposed checker speedup concerns commitment work only. A full verifier time has yet to be measured. [Build plan, §3](../../../docs/zkmatrix-poc/ZK60_SIZE_B6A_BUILD_PLAN_20261124.md#L232)
- **MODEL, B6b study.** Two wide-row 8B designs give 51.7 and 40.1 MB per stack request. These figures price neither the app's three category proofs nor an image edit or aggregate evaluation. D82 authorizes the B6a build. B6b remains a study. [Design variants](../../../docs/zkmatrix-poc/W16R3_DESIGN_B6_20261105.md#L295) · [D82](../../../docs/zkmatrix-poc/ZK60_DECISIONS.md#L133)
- **DERIVED transfer only.** At an assumed sustained 100 Mbit/s, seconds = decimal MB × 8 / 100. The guest archive takes **161 s** before verification, the 333.4 MB classifier request **26.7 s**, and the 40.1 MB study case **3.2 s**. At 10 Mbit/s multiply by ten. Actual network rates, mobile verification and participant time budgets were unmeasured.

## Fit by application

| Application | Who retains it and who checks it | Size step and remaining dependency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submission of unpublished writing | Author or authorized submission service proves locally. The editor's service downloads once per version, checks on a qualified workstation, then retains a signed result. An appeals reviewer can request the original receipt. | Today's size permits a small asynchronous trial where the editor budgets storage and transfer. B6a could cut that cost. An author's ordinary laptop still needs a qualified verifier or prover configuration as appropriate. The installed W16R2 classifier is for public content and its categories measure content safety. A private manuscript or machine-writing policy needs its own approved profile and quality study. [Mac measurements](20261124-apps-classifier-memory.md) · [Detector evidence](20261124-apps-text-detectors.md) |
| Peer review | The review platform could retain a receipt per submitted review or revision and give chairs access on audit. Requiring every referee to download proofs multiplies both transfer and checking time. | Central checking can trial today's transport or B6a. Inline attachments are a different constraint. **[Source's numbers]** NeurIPS 2025 limited main PDFs to 50 MB and supplements to 100 MB. That historical limit illustrates file size only. B6a exceeds even the larger figure for one classifier call. The B6b study merits a test for one small attachment. A multi-window review still needs aggregation or an external store. [Venue instructions](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers) |
| Captured image | The camera or author retains the original. An editor checks a signed original at intake. For a declared edit, the publisher could retain the proof and an independent reader fetch it on demand. | An unchanged signed file can use a hash and signature today. **MEASURED** synthetic 224 × 224 classifier proof, 2,873,106,100 bytes and 105.9 s of native replay. A full-resolution crop is a different statement. A newsroom workstation can test once-per-image storage. Browser delivery needs an image-specific receipt measurement and a much smaller proof for routine checks. The B6 text ratios cannot price it. [Image run](../../../results/zk60_astra_20261026/a4_decoder_recovery_20261123/white-20261124T073248.643451Z/replay.guard.json) · [Capture and edit requirements](20261124-apps-captured-image.md) |
| Private document checks | The sender's authorized gateway holds the clear attachment, checks each outgoing version and retains a private receipt. An auditor downloads selected receipts later on an authorized workstation. | A low-volume archive can test the existing sizes. **DERIVED scenario** of 10,000 one-request checks uses 6.70 TB at 669.6 MB each, or 3.33 TB at the B6a 333.4 MB target. Count extra policies and chunks separately. A high-volume gateway needs an explicit retention budget, a qualified private tagging model and a binding to the actual transfer. [Gateway workflow](20261124-apps-private-document-checks.md) |
| Hidden benchmark scores | The evaluator retains one receipt per prompt and checks each once. Independent researchers may download an entire run or ask for a single public aggregate. | **ESTIMATE** from the trained held-out 8B CPU run gives 772.1 GB for 1,000 receipts and 8.9 hours of serial checking. Today's evidence supports one-off institutional replay. Even the 40.1 MB B6b stack scenario scales to **40.1 GB** for 1,000 before token endpoints and scoring. A consumer-sized score receipt needs a proof of complete aggregation, and the hidden profile needs privacy review. [Evaluation evidence and arithmetic](20261124-apps-model-evaluations.md) |

**Deciding test.** For each pilot, agree on maximum bytes per item, daily retained volume, maximum time to a verified decision and the reader's hardware. With an empty cache and then a warm model kit, time actual download, rehash and native verification separately. Repeat with a multi-chunk item and a corrupted final segment. Recompute the table from complete receipts for each new proof profile. No B6a/B6b application receipt or policy acceptance was verified for this study.
