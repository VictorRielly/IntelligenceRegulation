# A19. What can survive a moderation decision

**Finding as of 25 November 2026.** There are two different six-month rules. Terrorist content covered by a removal order or specific measures must be preserved for six months. The DSA requires an effective complaint channel for at least six months but does not specify a blanket raw-content storage period. The old private-messaging derogation expired in April 2026. A distinct act, Regulation (EU) 2026/1881, entered into force on 31 July 2026 with a purpose-limited, maximum 12-month retention rule. That is not a minimum and does not cover ordinary public posts. [Terrorist content regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32021R0784) · [DSA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj/eng/pdf) · [2026/1881](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32026R1881)

| Terrorist content taken down under Regulation 2021/784, Arts. 3 or 5 | Preserve the content and necessary related data for review, complaints or terrorism enforcement. Restrict access and processing to these purposes. This is a specific preservation rule, not a rule for every political or harmful post. A receipt would not discharge it. | **Six months** from removal or disabling under Art. 6(2). Whether a copy is needed to handle redress is a separate, fact-specific question. | Art. 17 applies at the latest when the restriction is imposed if contact details are known, with express exceptions for deceptive high-volume commercial content and Art. 9 orders. Art. 20 sets **at least six months from notice** under Art. 16(5) or 17 for lodging complaints. **Which act applies.** Regulation 2021/1232 had the same 12-month ceiling. Regulation 2024/1307 extended it only through **3 April 2026**. The new act's recitals 10–13 record that expiry, and Art. 11 puts the new act in force from **31 July 2026**, applying until **3 April 2028**. Do not treat the two periods as continuous or apply the old rule to the intervening gap. The new act applies to specified number-independent interpersonal communications services, not openly published feeds or public posts. Its Art. 1 excludes audio scanning and end-to-end encrypted interpersonal communications. This does not remove obligations under other law or establish that every private platform feature qualifies. The permanent EU proposal is still listed as awaiting Parliament's first-reading position. ## Where a decision-time receipt adds something

A decision-time proof can remain the only **retained check of the model computation** when the raw item was lawfully deleted or never lawfully retained, no other authenticated copy remains, and retention of the proof itself is lawful. Those conditions can arise after a terrorism preservation period ends, after a covered messaging purpose ends, or on an ordinary moderation flow without a remaining lawful purpose to retain the full item. None of the laws says a receipt must survive, or that it is by itself a valid substitute for evidence a reviewer needs. A proof of a classifier run says which pinned model produced which result on the bound input. It does not recover the post, prove that the moderation was lawful, or establish a capture history without a separate trustworthy binding. When a preserve order applies, keep the original as required even if a proof exists.

**Test.** Use synthetic items for ordinary deletion, a timed preservation obligation and purpose-limited expiry. Generate the artifact while the input may lawfully be processed, then verify the private statement after scheduled deletion and reject a different model, policy or input binding. Keep the user notice and complaint path operational independently. If deleting the link makes it impossible to establish which item was actually reviewed, record that as an unresolved evidence gap. This is an EU-text comparison, not a Member State retention schedule or proof that any current profile satisfies the regulatory workflow.
