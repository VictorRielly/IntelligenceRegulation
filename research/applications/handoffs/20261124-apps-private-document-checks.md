# Evidence that a filter ran on a private document

The clearest use is an auditor checking a transfer between security domains. Where the applicable control calls for a content filter and an audit record, the owner could prove that the registered filter examined every token of a committed attachment and returned the recorded decision. The auditor would receive the authorized decision and proof. The owner would retain the attachment and opening. I compared the written requirements below with two released tagging models and counted one synthetic attachment using the installed tokenizer. [NIST controls AC-4(8) and AC-4(26)](https://pages.nist.gov/oscal-tools/demos/csx/baseline-reviewer/) · [NIST assessment procedures, AC-04(26)](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=933932)

### Where the evidence fits

| Workflow | What the outside reader needs | What the receipt could add |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cross-domain transfer or policy tagging | When selected, AC-4(8) uses organization-defined filters. AC-4(26) calls for records and audit of filtering actions and results. Neither selects a vendor or an ML model. | A versioned site policy can name the model, tag set and decision rule. A gateway-signed event must connect the checked attachment to the actual transfer, block or quarantine action. [NIST controls](https://pages.nist.gov/oscal-tools/demos/csx/baseline-reviewer/) |
| Personal data in a medical record | A conforming DICOM de-identifier records patient-identity removal and the method or profile used. HHS Expert Determination also requires a qualified expert to document the methods and results that justify a very small re-identification risk. | Prove a named text check on the outgoing free-text fields. A separate extraction and redaction relation is needed to connect those fields to the delivered file. Pixels, overlays, linked records and the expert’s contextual judgment fall outside a text tagger. [DICOM PS3.15 Annex E](https://dicom.nema.org/medical/dicom/current/output/chtml/part15/chapter_E.html) · [HHS guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/de-identification/index.html) |
| Privilege review and export records | A party asserting privilege must describe withheld material sufficiently for another party to assess the claim. BIS gives internal classification reasoning and the person who made the decision as examples of documentation worth keeping. | A receipt could document an agreed preliminary screen. Counsel must still support a privilege claim, and an export classification still needs item and transaction facts. Neither source requires a named model. I verified no open checkpoint that establishes either legal conclusion. [Court discovery handbook](https://www.flmd.uscourts.gov/civil-discovery-handbook/chapter06/a-invocation-of-privilege-or-other-protection) · [BIS recordkeeping guidance, Element 4](https://www.bis.gov/media/documents/export-compliance-guidelines) |

These sources identify audit or documentation obligations. The applicable access policy or agreement determines whether an auditor may see the original. The concrete opportunity is a site that already specifies both its filter and that access restriction.

### Models and one measured item

* **OpenAI Privacy Filter** identifies personal information in free text. [Source's number] It has 1.5B total parameters and 50M active parameters. It uses bidirectional attention and constrained Viterbi span decoding. A faithful integer port needs the routing, token classifier, label transitions and pinned decoding settings. Varying the transition settings can change which spans get flagged. [Authors' model card](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/c66281ed-b638-456a-8ce1-97e9f5264a90/OpenAI-Privacy-Filter-Model-Card.pdf)
* **Stanford de-identifier base** is a candidate for clinical free text. [Source's number] The published configuration has 12 BERT layers, width 768, an intermediate width of 3,072 and eight output labels including the background class. It names phone, patient and hospital labels and has no dedicated email label. DERIVED from the published dimensions, a BERT token classifier of that configuration has about 109M parameters including embeddings and the tag head. That is an architecture count. New proof relations would cover bidirectional attention, GELU, LayerNorm and token tags. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/StanfordAIMI/stanford-deidentifier-base) · [Published configuration](https://huggingface.co/StanfordAIMI/stanford-deidentifier-base/blob/main/config.json)

The synthetic attachment was the following single line, without a trailing newline.

> The release packet contains a single text attachment. Its review copy lists the contact address as sample@example.invalid. The reviewer is to record the policy version and whether a contact detail was detected.

COUNTED, 210 UTF-8 bytes, 32 whitespace-delimited words and 38 private-content tokens using the pinned Tekken encoder's leading-space convention. MEASURED on the Mac, the six-lane trained 3B W16R2 classifier achieved 48.6–50.0 padded tokens/s in a stream of independent requests. DERIVED, one padded 128-position check at that rate consumes 128/50.0 to 128/48.6 = **2.56–2.63 seconds of steady-state capacity**. The timed interval excludes receipt verification. This is a sizing reference for a single check. Both taggers need their own tokenizer, complete port, calibration and timing before there is a per-document latency estimate. The installed three policies screen moderation categories and the profile is restricted to public input. ### A deciding test

Have the holder commit locally with a fresh salt to a synthetic original and the exact outgoing attachment. Pin the extraction, tokenizer, weights, integer scales, policy and span decoder. Prove full token coverage and one agreed result, such as “the email tag occurred.” Keep spans and token scores private. Use examples with an address at the last token and across a chunk boundary. Reject a missing chunk, reordered attachment, different policy or different transfer identifier. Independently measure false negatives on representative held-out documents before setting an acceptance threshold. To claim every transfer was checked, reconcile a gateway-issued sequence or signed inventory with the receipts. A per-item proof alone cannot count omitted transfers. Private use also requires acceptance of the deployed privacy profile. No confidential source document or ported-model proof was used in this study.
