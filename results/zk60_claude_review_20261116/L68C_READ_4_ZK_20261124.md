  `prog` and its tie to the prover (`hprog`, 71-72) are witness fields. The central obligation `hout` (156-158) is

  view. The top theorems take the whole witness as the premise `W_zk`. **What settles it:** build a term of type
  `ShdZKProgWitness … (shdP_honestRunP …) …`, so that `W_zk` disappears from the tops. The Top header admits this
  is open (Top.lean:16-20).

### Medium

- **M1 (satisfiability not shown; ZK.lean:89-90 with ZKHonestProg.lean:200-201).** `hkind` requires the kind of
  every point index to be independent of coins and oracle values. `ptsOf` fills the slots after an early stop with
  pad points of kind `.tr`. A program that stops early on a refusal that depends on oracle values therefore fails
  `hkind`, unless it keeps making dummy queries of the right kinds. Also, `hcntT` is exact: 283,879 transcript points
  plus 764,697 trailing pads = 2^20 (ZKProg:1416-1426). So any leaf or node miscount breaks it. Until a value is
  built, the tops could be vacuously true. **What settles it:** build `W_zk`. Failing that, show that the honest
  program has a fixed query shape.
- **M2 (carried as M2; ZKProg:1402-1426, ZKCount:76-91).** Tree counts, depths, the transcript counts 605, 10,862
  and 797, and the header count 7 are literals. ZKCount proves that they sum consistently. It does not derive them
  from `shdP_honestRunP`.
