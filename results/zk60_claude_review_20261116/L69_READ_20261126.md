## Item 6: the two model differences
- **(a)** Confirmed, and extended to `wholeRunBits` (M1).
- **(b)** Confirmed (M2). For each part:
  - **Soundness / binding.** Proved for a verifier that accepts more. A verifier that also makes the challenge
    refusals accepts a subset, so the bounds transfer by monotonicity. That step is NOT stated in Lean.
  - **Completeness.** The Lean proof needs the prover-side guard only in the direction "guarded success ⇒ pure check
    passes". Against the Lean verifier the guard costs completeness (more refusals, covered by the sites). Against a
    verifier that does check, completeness needs the guard: the prover then refuses exactly where that check would
    fail on the prover's transcript. No Lean theorem states completeness for such a verifier. The Rust prover does
    run the check before every boundary opening (`into_opening` → `check_opening`, boundary.rs:275-280). READ. That
    this covers B_0..25 of the classifier prover is INFERRED.
  - **ZK.** Independent of the verifier. It needs the prover guard (FixedOps.lean:1-9, "so `PcsStdData.hab`/`hp`
    hold"), and that guard is in the proved prover.
