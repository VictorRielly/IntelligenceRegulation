READING, control by control.
 * ONE zero cm or lambda kills the minor taken at THAT member's pair but
   not k0: another member of the same group still carries the group.  The
   lemma therefore needs, per group, ONE member with lambda*cm != 0, and
   the code refuses when ANY of them is zero -- strictly stronger.
 * EVERY cm of a group zero is the real danger: the group vector is then
   nonzero on the data rows and zero on every non-data row, i.e. a bare
   functional of the hidden data.  k0 jumps to 2 (512 Fp disclosed).
   audit_claims' zero-coefficient refusal is what forbids it.
 * EVERY lambda of a group zero leaves k0 = 0: the group vector is then
   identically zero, which discloses nothing.  The refusal on lambda = 0
   is therefore conservative for THIS event (it is there for soundness).
 * A dependent proximity pair also leaves k0 = 0 at this shape: the
   proximity vector is random on the other 104 non-data rows and keeps
   its two generators independent there.  The refusal is again
   conservative -- but the u-row argument of section 3.3 does use it.
 * Two claims sharing one reserved pair is the one control that breaks
   the rank badly (k0 = 16 here), and audit_claims refuses it.
