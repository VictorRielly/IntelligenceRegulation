READING
  * every honest case reaches rank 2(G+1) on its non-data rows, so k0 = 0;
  * where the SLACK (non-data rows minus generators) is zero, a refusal that
    the large commitments can absorb becomes load-bearing.  The BOUNDARY has
    slack 0 at every shape, and its proximity refusal is therefore EXACTLY
    NECESSARY: with an Fp-dependent proximity pair k0 rises.  On `A` and `H`,
    with slack in the hundreds, the same perturbation leaves k0 at 0 — which
    is what the toy's check found, and it is a fact about the slack, not
    about the refusal;
  * the endpoint's FIFTH (spare) row is what gives it slack 1: remove it and
    the dependent proximity pair moves k0 there too.
