   Needs first: a 1-2 minute demo video or screen recording, a one-line pitch and the

   the program offers help forming the company.

   be checked without being trusted (private moderation verdicts, AI audits), with
   numbers measured on a laptop. Needs first: **a text Victor writes himself**. Agent

   Name the ask (for example a GPU box or 6 months of runway).

   safety-classifier verdicts and private inference audits as alignment and oversight
   tooling. Needs first: a short form and a Claude Console org. The credits cover API
   use only, not Claude.ai.
4. **Anthropic Fellows, AI Safety & Security** (deadline Oct 30). Apply only if Victor would
   commit 4 months full-time and accept that fellows' work is research (IP terms are not
   stated). Pitch: verifiable/private inference for audits and control evaluations.
   Needs first: a decision within 48 hours, US work authorization and a CV.
5. **1517 Medici grant** (now). Pitch: a deep-tech scientist validating private verifiable
   AI. Needs first: the contact form. The money is small but fast.

7. **OpenAI Cybersecurity Grant** (rolling, Partial). Pitch: privacy-preserving, verifiable
   content moderation and AI audit trails as defensive infrastructure. Needs first: a

   solo builder with measured results. Needs first: decide how to describe the venture
   plan. OSV sends VC-track founders to Infinite Adventures, its venture arm.
9. **Cosmos grants, next round** (interest form now). Pitch: "AI for truth-seeking", where
   anyone can check a claim about what a classifier concluded without seeing the content.
   Needs first: the interest form and a 90-day prototype scope.
10. **Astera Residency, next cycle** (dates unknown; about twice a year). Pitch: the open
    verifier, spec and benchmarks as public goods, using Astera's H100/B200 fleet for the
    scaling measurements. Needs first: a ruling on what can be opened. Astera expects open
    work, though commercial terms "can be negotiated".
11. **Lower priority, low effort:** a Convergent FRO idea (an open verifier and benchmark
    suite for private AI audits); a Hugging Face GPU grant for a public verifier demo (only
    after the provisional patent is filed); MATS Summer 2027 notifications; Claude for

demo or Space; (b) the Delaware C-corp plus a domain and website, which unlock every cloud
