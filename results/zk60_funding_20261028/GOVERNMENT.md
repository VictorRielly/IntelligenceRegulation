# Government (non-dilutive) funding for the private-LLM ZK prover

Checked 2026-10-28 (US/UK/EU government programs open to a US applicant). Research only: nothing was applied for, registered or sent. Every row was checked against an official page where one could be reached. "UNVERIFIED" means only secondary or aggregator sources were found. "Estimate" marks my own timing judgement.

## 1. Plain summary

- **No government program pays within ~3 months.** Every federal route needs a registered entity, and reviews alone take months.
- **Most dependable money: NSF SBIR Phase I, up to $305,000.** Fast-Track goes up to about $1.555M. The topic areas fit squarely: CA3 "Data Privacy and Integrity", CA9 "Secure and Trusted Computing", AI7 "Trustworthy AI ... privacy-preserving". Victor can submit the 4-section Project Pitch **this week with no company and no registrations**. The reply takes 1–2 months. The 2026-11-04 deadline cannot be met without an invitation already in hand, so the first realistic full-proposal deadline is **2027-03-04**. A decision would come about 6 months later, so money around **Sept–Oct 2027** (estimate).
- **Biggest and possibly fastest: DARPA IPTO Office-Wide BAA HR001126S0011.** Abstracts are reviewed on a rolling basis until 2027-06-22. Two named thrusts fit: "Inherent Security and Privacy" and assured, trustworthy AI. No ceiling is published, and US and non-US sources are both eligible. If a program manager is interested, an award 4–9 months out is plausible (estimate). The bar is "revolutionary, not evolutionary".
- **DoD SBIR:** the FY27 Release 1 topics (open now, close 2026-11-25) include nothing on cryptography, ZK or AI assurance. Watch Release 2 (topics shown 2026-11-04, closes 2027-01-06) and Release 3 (closes 2027-02-03).
- **Every federal award requires an entity.** SBIR needs a for-profit US small business at least 51% owned by US citizens or permanent residents, with the PI more than 50% employed there. DARPA and ONR need an active SAM.gov UEI. Victor cannot win any of these as an individual.
- **Foreign programs are effectively closed to this project.** UK AISI grants are closed and the Challenge Fund is for academic and non-profit applicants only. NLnet Restack excludes AI projects and needs a European dimension. NGI Zero Commons has closed for good. Innovate UK and Horizon Europe do not fund US companies.

## 3. Top 5 (fit × speed × amount)

**1. NSF SBIR Phase I or Fast-Track (CA9 / CA3 / AI7).** Highest fit and the clearest rules.
- **Next step:** submit the Project Pitch now. Frame it as private-LLM-inference proofs, with trained 8B next-token proofs already on a laptop. Ask the program director whether Fast-Track fits.
- **Needs first:** none for the pitch. Before the full proposal:
  - form a for-profit LLC or C-corp: state filing plus EIN, ~1–7 days (estimate);
  - SAM.gov registration, financial-assistance only: "up to three weeks" per NSF;
  - Research.gov company registration: ≤48 h;
  - SBA company registry: SBC ID.
- **Eligibility to confirm:** ownership ≥51% by US citizens or permanent residents, and Victor >50% employed by the company at award.
- Start SAM as soon as an invitation looks likely. It is the critical path for 2027-03-04.

**2. DARPA IPTO Office-Wide BAA.** Largest upside, rolling intake.
- **Next step:** email an IPTO PM in Inherent Security & Privacy or assured AI (the BAA "strongly encourages" this). Then send the abstract on the required IPTO template. Pitch it as revolutionary: hidden-activation proofs of a full 8B model, with a path to ≥50 proved tokens/s.
- **Needs first:** an entity with an active SAM UEI before proposing, and the FRRBS foreign-influence forms for grants or OTs. Ask for a grant or OT, not a procurement contract, to avoid CMMC Level 2.
- About 1 month to be proposal-ready if SAM starts now (estimate).

**3. DoD SBIR FY27 Release 2 / 3.** Possibly faster money than NSF if a matching topic appears.
- **Next step:** read the Release 2 topics on 2026-11-04 and Release 3 on 2026-12-02. Look for cryptography, AI assurance, trusted computing or content authentication. During pre-release, questions to topic authors are allowed.
- **Needs first:** entity; SAM with "all awards" (contracts) plus a CAGE code; SBIR.gov SBC control ID, whose identity verification "can take extra time"; a DSIP account. Plan 3–5 weeks.

**4. ONR Long Range BAA (proposals by 2026-12-31) and the ARL/ARO BAA (STIR).**
- **Next step:** a 1–2 page white paper to an ONR Code 31 (cyber/AI) program officer or an ARO computing-sciences TPOC before December.
- **Needs first:** entity plus SAM. The ONR deadline is tight. ARL stays open longer.

**5. NSF SaTC 2.0 through a university partner (target ~2027-01-25).** This also enables NSF STTR.
- **Next step:** find a crypto or ZK faculty PI who will lead a SEED (≤$300K) or RES proposal with Victor as collaborator or subawardee. The same partner can be the STTR co-PI.
- **Needs first:** the partner's agreement and the university's sponsored-programs lead time, typically weeks (estimate).
- Watch NIST SBIR FY27 (expected early 2027) as an alternate.

## 4. Pitfalls

- **Entity and SAM are the critical path.** SAM is free. Ignore paid "registration" services, and match the IRS name and address exactly. NSF warns of UEI backlogs and up to 3 weeks; practitioners report 3–4 weeks or more. SAM must be renewed every year. NSF needs financial-assistance registration; DoD contracts need "all awards".
- **SBIR eligibility:**
  - Not open to individuals or non-profits.
  - Ownership ≥51% by US citizens or permanent residents. A firm majority-owned by VC funds is ineligible at NSF. Any later raise could break this.
  - PI more than 50% employed by the company at award; another job over 19.6 h/week is a conflict.
  - All R&D must be done in the US. That rules out offshore contractors and possibly non-US cloud GPUs (confirm with the program).
- **New 2026 law (S.3971) changes:**
  - FY2027 proposal caps per company: NSF allows 2 pitches per 12 months and 3 per technology.
  - Expanded foreign-risk screening of owners and key personnel.
  - DoD's due-diligence review is now the "Foreign Risk Evaluation Program".
  - A malign foreign talent program bars a person outright.
- **Deadlines:** the NSF 2026-11-04 deadline is out of reach without an existing invitation. An NSF invitation lasts only two deadlines.
- **DARPA:**
  - "Evolutionary improvements" are excluded; a speed-up pitch alone will fail.
  - CUI requires CMMC L2 systems.
  - DARPA chooses whether the work counts as fundamental research, which affects publication.
- **Claims:** the soundness and privacy statements are accepted internally only, with no external audit. Say so in proposals. Lead with measured numbers (8B prove 192 s, verify 43 s), not targets.
- **In-Q-Tel** submissions carry no NDA and may be shared with IC customers; send nothing confidential. It takes equity.
- **Open-source conditions:** NLnet requires a FOSS licence. Decide the licensing of the prover before any EU or open-source grant.
- **Challenge.gov was retired 2026-03-30 (secondary).** Prize calls now live only on each agency's own site.
