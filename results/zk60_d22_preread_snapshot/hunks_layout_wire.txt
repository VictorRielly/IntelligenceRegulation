N1 (note, not a finding). The toy FREEZE GATE (Layout::new, snap:1032-1054) is
NOT on the prover's or the verifier's own call: protocol::prove_inner_at
(protocol.rs:676) and verify_inner_at (protocol.rs:1171) call
Layout::for_shape(&Shape::toy()) directly. The gate runs in the toy's public
codec and helpers — Receipt::to_bytes / from_bytes (private_block.rs:935,
940), encoded_len (:929), schedule (:983) — so verify_bytes and every
serialised toy proof pass through it. Since for_shape(Shape::toy()) is a pure
function of compile-time constants, one execution anywhere establishes it, and
the lane's toy tests execute it; but a caller that proves and verifies
in-memory Receipt structs (prove_with_stats + verify) never executes the gate.
All values it asserts were re-derived by hand above.

(constants T/D/H/DH/HALF/KV/F/LOG_N/N, NORM_*, NORM_WRHO 29, F_E/F_P/F_RO,
WRHO_S 14, MASK_SCORE, every A_* / H_* id, N_A, N_LIMBS), `_block_lookups`
(the literal lists of main layout.rs:310-368), `helpers` (:371) and pow256
(:690). Every one of those VALUES is unchanged at the toy (constants

constants block runs to snap:~455. The port transcribes the OLD literal form;
