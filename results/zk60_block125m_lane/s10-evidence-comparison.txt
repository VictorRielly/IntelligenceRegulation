STAGE 10 -- the inventory tool's record, before and after the shape generalisation
=================================================================================

WHAT THIS IS.  `scripts/zk60_private_block_inventory.py` derives the toy block's layout by
PARSING hashline/src/private_block/{layout,argument,protocol}.rs.  The lane's steps A..C
rewrote those files over a `Shape`, and nine tests of the tool began to ERROR (finding S9-F6).
Stage 10 taught the parser the new source structure.  The acceptance test for that repair is
this file: the tool's own record, regenerated from the shaped source, compared KEY BY KEY with
the record committed at f1df3ba, which was made from the pre-lane source.

A = results/zk60_private_block_tools/ as committed at f1df3ba (the pre-lane record)
B = the same directory regenerated on zk60-private-block-125m, stage 10

Every DERIVED quantity is identical: every count, every claim site, every opening group, every
row index, every rank verdict, every CFS verdict, every negative control, the whole schedule
and the paper comparison.  `zk60_private_block_{schedule,pair_schedule,cfs_slots,hosts}.json`
are identical in every key.  The differences below are, without exception:

  * the three `source_sha256` entries for the Rust files the lane rewrote (twice each, in
    the inventory and the summary);
  * `adapters` and `adapters_used_note`, the tool's own PROSE: five adapter entries re-pinned
    to the new line numbers and four new entries B-7..B-10;
  * `adapters_used_by_the_define_parser` (and its copy inside the findings): B-2 is gone,
    because the spelling it adapted (`lay.helpers(..)`) no longer occurs anywhere in
    argument.rs, and B-9 / B-10 take its place;
  * `logup_adapter_record/h_planes_body`, which quotes the source line that moved, and the
    added `is_table_body`;
  * four ADDED summary keys: `define_rewrites`, `layout_adapter_record`,
    `toy_profile_read_from_the_rust`, `independent_model_cross_check`;
  * one ADDED gate (11 -> 12, hence `findings[1]/detail` "11 of 11 pass" -> "12 of 12 pass"):
    the toy profile read out of the shaped Rust against the independent layout model;
  * `out_dir` / `outputs`, which name the worktree the run happened in.
