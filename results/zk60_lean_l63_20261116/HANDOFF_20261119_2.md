## 6. Pitfalls met this leg

- **`match` on the table index does not reduce at symbolic indices** (`g (15 + kk)`, `g (2 + r)` inside sums): read the
  tables through the whole list (`stgΦB_sa_evalL`), and shift with `fun idx => … .getD (idx - 1) 0` plus
  `dzcPairG_congr` (`(j + 1) - 1` reduces to `j`, `rfl`).
- **`set` after `subst` can shadow a local** whose type mentions the abstracted term (the cube point `c` became
  `c✝`): `revert c` before the `set`s, `intro c` after (as the TAB/LZC proofs do).
- **A `first | exact … | exact …` over goals whose terms differ only in `O.llo` / `O.ldo`** makes Lean unfold
  `clog2` to compare them and times out: prove such facts by cases (`interval_cases p` and one `show` per case).
- The 18-part `logupSumClaim` list by plain `rfl` timed out; `show` its `flatMap` form, rewrite `hLog.length = 9`
  and `List.range 9`, `simp only [List.flatMap_cons, …]`, then `rfl`.
- `rw` with a hypothesis stated at `(pIdx1 k).val` does not match `2 * ↑k` in the goal: restate it at `2 * k.val` by a
  type ascription first.
- When a lemma's `poly` is left as `_`, it is read off `hdef` as `tabPoly … hist.alpha` (a structure projection), and
  the `toK` rewrites then miss: pass `poly` explicitly with the `alpha` the rewrites use.
- `ring`/`linear_combination` atoms must match syntactically; do not abbreviate the table values by `set` before a
  `linear_combination` against an expanded form.
- `unfold stgPlB` on a partially applied `stgPlB … v` leaves a beta-redex: `funext b; show … v b = … v' b` first.
