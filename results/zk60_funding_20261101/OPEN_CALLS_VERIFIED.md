## 3. Program notes (fast or urgent first)

**Anthropic External Researcher Access.** Status: rolling; "We evaluate
submissions on the first Monday of each month" (V). Next reviews: Mon 2 Nov
2026 and Mon 7 Dec 2026 (dates computed from that rule). Amount: "$1000 API
credits", rarely more. Eligible: anyone doing safety or alignment research;
needs a Claude Console organization ID before submitting. Publicity: the form
says submissions are treated "as non-confidential". Fit Med: credits pay for
agent-driven engineering, but it is not cash. Form: `FORM_FIELDS.md` §1.

**NLnet.** Deadline "November 3rd 2026 12:00 CET (noon)" (V). That is 03:00
PST on Nov 3. €5k-€50k. Individuals may apply. EU funds need a "strong
European dimension" (team members in Horizon Europe countries receiving ≥60%
of funds), unless "exceptional quality". Restack: "AI-related projects are not
within scope" unless >1 million users. **New since 10-28:** NLnet's 20 Oct
2026 policy says "Generative AI can no longer be used for writing proposals"
and in project work "the use of LLMs and diffusion models is confined to
read-only usage". This project's code is written largely by AI agents, so it
does not fit. Fit Low. Form (verbatim, for completeness): `FORM_FIELDS.md` §2.

**Mozilla Human-Scale AI Incubator.** Deadline "Friday, November 20, 2026 at
11:59 PM Pacific Standard Time (UTC-8)" (V, on the form). $300k; up to $500k
over three years (P). Cohort "will run for 12 months beginning in April 2027";
you confirm you can "participate fully" (V). Mozilla's general eligibility
guide says applicants are 501(c)(3)s or equivalents and "For-profit social
enterprises will be considered on a case-by-case basis" (V, PDF); the
incubator form still offers "Individual" and "Fiscal sponsorship" (V).
Outputs must be openly licensed. The form asks the project stage and user
count, whether the core depends on a frontier-model API, and the largest
parameter count; it names "GPT, Claude, Gemini/DeepMind, Llama" as frontier
families. Fit Low: no users yet, and the Llama-shaped model may trip the
"slimmed-down frontier model" exclusion. Form: §3.

**Lightcone Commons.** Rolling. "The next funding round will close
mid-December"; "it needs to be submitted 3 weeks before the close of the
round"; "We expect evaluators to have completed their recommendations by
December 1st" (V). So submit by about Nov 15 to be read before Dec 1, and no
later than about Nov 24 (est.). Monthly rounds after that. Grants to
"501c3s, individuals, and for-profit organizations"; individuals get fiscal
sponsorship if the work is charitable. Decision "usually within two weeks of
the round closing"; money "within a month after that". The application is
visible to funders, evaluators and direct grantors, who "might also choose to
share your application with external references or third parties ...
including sharing the fact that you applied publicly". Agreements have "few
constraints outside of annual reporting". It may import Manifund and LTFF
applications. Fit High: the main funders (Tallinn, Moskovitz, LTFF, ARM Fund)
back AI-safety work. Form: §4 (behind a login).

**BlueDot Seed Grants (new find).** Rolling. "$20k-$200k for 6-12 months";
"We aim to decide within about 4 weeks of your application" (V). "Right now
we pay Seed Grants to individual founders." For-profit: "In some cases, where
the work clearly serves the public good ... These grants take longer to
approve." "Solo founders are welcome." Allowed uses include living costs to
work full-time, compute and travel; full-time is not stated as required.
Light reporting (monthly short update, final report). The form takes about 45
minutes; choose "Starting an organization". Fit High: fast, sizeable, and it
introduces grantees to Coefficient Giving and Longview. Form: §5 (not
readable; JavaScript).

**BlueDot Rapid Grants.** Rolling. "Up to $20k"; "About 15 minutes to apply";
