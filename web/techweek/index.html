    What this proves today
    These are fixed, small, synthetic model profiles. The Groth16 receipts cover hidden inputs, outputs and activations under the project's recorded assumptions. The live native example uses a public fixture and demonstrates proof generation and verification.

    What funding moves forward
    Trained-model calibration and integration, broader privacy validation, and measured GPU performance. The target is a complete private 8B stack at ≥50 proved tokens/s. That target has not been demonstrated.

    Proof assumptions and scope

    Groth16 uses a trusted setup and the accepted underlying zkVM soundness assumption (about 97 conjectured bits). Endpoint hiding relies on secret salts. The program images are locally built, unreproduced; timing is declared leakage. Public weights are allowed. The model is untrained, and successful verification is not an approval of a model's safety or quality.
